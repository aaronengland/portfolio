Gen 12 Model Monitoring

Model monitoring is systematically conducted during the loan origination process to observe the Gen 12 model over time. This discerns and diagnoses factors contributing to potential model degradation and limitations in generalization capabilities. Model monitoring serves to signify instances necessitating the refreshment or reconstruction of models, as well as adjustments to policies, approval rates, and tier thresholds.
