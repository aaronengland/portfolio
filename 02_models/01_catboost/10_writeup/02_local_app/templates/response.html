SHAP Values

Adverse action reasons in lending provide individuals with explanations for why their application was declined, ensuring transparency and compliance with regulatory requirements such as the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA). These reasons detail specific factors or criteria that influenced the lender's decision, empowering applicants to address concerns, rectify issues, and potentially improve their chances in future applications.

In the response for each applicant, five adverse action reasons are provided, aligning with specific features. Identifying the five most impactful features on the model score is imperative. To ascertain these influential features, the methodology of Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP) values was applied. These values offer a quantified assessment of the individual impact of each feature on the model's output, attributing contributions to predictions based on feature values.

Once the top five influential features are identified, they are matched with their respective reasons. These resultant five reasons are subsequently included in the response. For applicants that are declined based on their score on the AD model, adverse action reasons are mapped to SHAP values from the AD model. Otherwise, reasons are mapped to SHAP values from the pricing PD model.
